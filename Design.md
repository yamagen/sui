SUI design invariants

1. SUI does not generate glosses.
2. Analyses come from attested records in the ledger.
3. Ambiguity is preserved unless the data resolve it.
4. Unknown input must not be silently analyzed by inference.
5. Trie, lattice, etc. are implementation mechanisms,
   not linguistic assumptions.
6. Do not add ranking, scoring, or disambiguation
   without an explicit design decision.
